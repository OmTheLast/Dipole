How to read the map and its uncertainty

Each color represents a cell’s estimated rate, in °C per decade. We fit a straight line through 46 annual averages and multiply the rate by 4.5 decades to estimate the total change. Each year requires all 12 months. Gray cells have incomplete records.

The 95% interval describes uncertainty in the fitted trend for one selected place. It accounts for similar temperatures in nearby years using Newey–West standard errors with three annual lags. It excludes additional uncertainty in NASA’s temperature estimates. Testing many locations can produce chance findings. Map colors show the size of the trend.

Region buttons use area-weighted averages. Each map click selects a single 2° cell. NASA combines land air and open-ocean surface temperatures and estimates values between measurement sites.
